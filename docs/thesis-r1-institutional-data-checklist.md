# R1: institutional data requirements checklist

2026-11-02, Asia/Manila. **Request for evidence, not populated institutional data.** IT is the available prototype scope. No institutionally approved complete evaluation dataset or authority package is established by this checklist. See the [adviser package](thesis-r1-adviser-decision-package.md) and [32-item technical register](thesis-r1-implementation-readiness.md).

## Acceptance and custody rules

For every supplied source, require an accountable owner, exact scope/year/term, stable identifiers, source revision and content hash, effective dates, completeness statement, verification/approval status and evidence reference, authorised verifier/approver and mandate, correction/supersession/revocation rules, and access/retention restrictions. A hash proves bytes, not institutional approval. A dataset delivered by the adviser is useful provenance, not permission to infer all these facts.

Use **EXISTS/PARTIAL**, **NOT REVIEWED**, **VERIFICATION PENDING**, **ACCEPTED FOR SPECIFIED SCOPE**, or **NOT APPLICABLE WITH APPROVED REASON**. R1 does not assign the accepted status to an institutional source. Preserve unknown separately from explicitly attested empty/zero/none. Private reconciliation must not place faculty names, credential copies, preference rows, authentication data or passwords in public evidence or chat.

## Reconciliation priorities

1. Identify the term, official roster/workload revision and approved curriculum before treating counts as a complete study population. Current registry agreement is not roster completeness. No automatic union of old preference, qualification and load files is acceptable.
2. Privately reconcile stable IDs, missing load/profile fields and eligibility sources with the responsible owners. F1.5's numeric refinement distinguishes one comparable workload disagreement from unknown/nonnumeric pairs; do not reclassify raw text differences as verified academic conflicts.
3. Obtain controlled specialization definitions and evidence. Existing tags do not decide who is authorised to teach. Explicit eligibility and specialization measurement need independent verification.
4. Obtain complete real resource calendars and confirmed outside commitments. One physical room or shared IT participant must have one identity across departments. Record required-but-unresolved resources instead of silently dropping them.
5. Confirm real capability mandates and private research permissions. Then lock a coherent dataset/protocol revision for evaluation; later changes create a new reviewed revision rather than overwrite the original.

Counts above are **reviewed historical source findings** from F1.5/F3A/F3C, whose files remain unchanged; R1 performs no fresh institutional row parsing. No real identity, qualification document, workload value, preference or source row is reproduced here.

## Suggested private submission record

For each DI item, the custodian should record: applicability, source owner and contact route, stable dataset ID/version/hash, approved year/term/department, completeness and unresolved cases, verifier/approver mandate and decision reference, effective/expiry dates, correction/revocation linkage, permitted research use/access/retention, and the R1 decisions it resolves. Keep the actual data and sensitive evidence in institutionally approved restricted custody. Submit only approved deidentified aggregates to research reports.

Acceptance must be **for a specific scope and purpose**: accepted for structural inspection, accepted as a teaching-permission source and accepted for a particular comparison are different claims. Data acceptance is not automatic publication authority or production activation. All institutional decisions and populated data remain outside this documentation milestone.
